About Me

For the past 14 years, I have built software across startups and established companies — spanning web applications, backend systems and infrastructure. Currently, I'm a systems engineer at Clearhaus, managing and developing payment systems.

I've also worked at Peanut (social network), GoMore (ride-sharing), Skyscanner (travel planning), HouseTrip (holiday rentals), Evently (event management).

My drive comes from delivering value to the business and enjoyment to its users and customers. Beyond web applications, I've built and shipped work in marketing automation, finance, analytics, mobile development and DevOps — and led small teams along the way. I like picking up whatever language or tool a problem calls for rather than sticking to one stack.

You can read more about my career on LinkedIn.
